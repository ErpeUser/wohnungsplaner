Feature

  • 360° SWIVEL SEAT: Thanks to the 360° swivel mechanism, you can turn the dining room swivel chair effortlessly and freely in any direction. In addition, the roll-free construction increases safety during use.
  • WIDE SEAT: The wide seat is covered with skin-friendly and soft velvet and padded with elastic foam, so that you can feel comfortable and relax even when you sit for a long time.
  • ERGONOMIC CHAIR: With the armrests and backrest, the dining chair provides a comfortable dining experience while supporting your lumbar spine. It can also be used as a kitchen chair, living room chair or dressing table chair.
  • ROBUST & STABLE: Thanks to the stable swivel base and the black painted metal legs, the chair is robust and can be loaded up to 150 kg. The 4 foot pads prevent slipping and scratches on the floor, 2 of them are adjustable for more stability.
  • EASY ASSEMBLY: With the illustrated instructions, you can assemble this wide upholstered chair with armrests step by step, and then enjoy your leisure time in no time.
